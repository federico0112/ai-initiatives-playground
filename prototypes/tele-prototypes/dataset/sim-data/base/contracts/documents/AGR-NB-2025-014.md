# Wholesale Voice Termination Agreement (NBCS-WVT-2025-0117)

Internal ID AGR-NB-2025-014, version 2 (amendment A1 added Guatemala Mobile, 2026-02-01). Fictional, prototype use only.
Between **Northbridge Carrier Services** ("Seller") and **Orion Voice Exchange** ("Buyer"). Signed 2025-02-20.

<a id="clause-1.1"></a>
1.1 **Parties and term.** Agreement between NBCS (seller) and ORX (buyer), effective 2025-03-01, evergreen.

<a id="clause-3.1"></a>
3.1 **Currency.** All charges in USD.

<a id="clause-4.1"></a>
4.1 **Billing period.** Monthly; calls attributed to a period by start time in UTC.

<a id="clause-4.2"></a>
4.2 **Billing increment.** 60/60: first 60 seconds, then 60-second increments.

<a id="clause-4.3"></a>
4.3 **Charge rounding.** Line amount = billable minutes x rate, rounded half-up to 2 decimals per invoice line.

<a id="clause-5.1"></a>
5.1 **Invoicing.** Invoice issued within 5 days after period end, with summary by destination; CDR detail on request.

<a id="clause-5.2"></a>
5.2 **Payment.** Payment due 30 days from invoice date.

<a id="clause-6.1"></a>
6.1 **Rate decks.** Seller prices from the NB-VOICE rate deck family; decks may be full or partial.

<a id="clause-6.2"></a>
6.2 **Change indicators.** Every changed row carries a change flag and effective date.

<a id="clause-6.3"></a>
6.3 **Rate increase notice.** Seller shall give Buyer at least seven (7) days' written notice of any rate increase or new destination. An increase takes effect at 00:00 UTC on the first full day that begins at least seven (7) days after the notice is sent, or on the effective date stated in the deck if later. Where a deck states an earlier date, the previous rate continues to apply until the increase takes effect under this clause.

<a id="clause-6.4"></a>
6.4 **Rate decreases.** A decrease takes effect on the stated effective date, with no notice period.

<a id="clause-8.1"></a>
8.1 **Dispute window.** Buyer may dispute any invoice, in whole or in part, by written notice within fourteen (14) days of the invoice date.

<a id="clause-8.2"></a>
8.2 **Minimum dispute.** Disputes below USD 50.00 per invoice are not accepted.

<a id="clause-8.3"></a>
8.3 **Undisputed amounts.** Buyer shall pay the undisputed portion of an invoice by its due date. Raising a dispute does not suspend payment of undisputed amounts.

<a id="clause-8.4"></a>
8.4 **Dispute evidence.** Notice states billed rate, correct rate, period, account ID and supporting CDR detail.

<a id="clause-8.5"></a>
8.5 **Escalation.** Disputes unresolved after 30 days escalate to commercial management.

<a id="clause-8.6"></a>
8.6 **Credits.** Upheld disputes are credited on the next invoice.
