Prototype 2 · Operations Resolution Agents

SMS delivery incident flow

How one case moves from an alert that delivery to Mexico / Altavia Movil fell from 94% to 68% to an evidence-backed supplier escalation and a verified recovery. The system shows competing causes when records conflict, waits when the evidence is not in yet, and never changes routes or contacts a supplier without an operations decision.

rules deterministic code, same input gives same output agent model reasoning, bounded by cited evidence analyst operations decision outside our customer's systems, or a paused state

Guardrails

  • Every number comes from a rules calculation in the ledger; agents cite it and never introduce a new one.
  • Records are read as of the simulated clock, so a receipt that has not arrived is unknown, not failed.
  • No route change and no external message without an operations decision. Containment is a recommendation.
  • Closing needs verified recovery on the data, whatever the supplier says.
  • The demo measures brief quality and time to prepare it, not real-world time to repair.
